How these numbers are calculated
Voice
  • Inbound Volume — calls that reached a queue, including callbacks requested through the phone menu. Calls that resolve in the menu without reaching a queue are not counted.
  • Answered — calls where an agent connected.
  • % Answered ≤30s and Avg Speed of Answer — live calls only. A callback’s wait is the time until we ring back rather than hold time, so including it would distort both.
  • Abandonment — counted in patients, not calls: of the patients who reached a queue, the share who never connected with an agent and never received a callback attempt. Each patient counts once however many times they call.
Email
  • Received / Resolved / Net — tickets in, tickets closed, and Received minus Resolved.
  • First Response ≤4hr / ≤24hr — share of tickets whose first reply went out within that window, measured from when the email arrived.
  • Open backlog — tickets still open at the end of the window.
All figures cover the stated window, Central Time.
